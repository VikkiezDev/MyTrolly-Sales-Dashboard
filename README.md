# 📊 MyTrolly Sales Performance & Revenue Analytics Dashboard

An interactive, end-to-end Excel Sales Dashboard designed to track key performance metrics, regional revenue distribution, sales channel performance, and product category trends across 2,000+ transactions.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Dynamic KPI Scorecard:** Real-time visibility into key business metrics:
  * **Total Revenue:** $2.45M
  * **Total Orders:** 2,000
  * **Quantity Sold:** 5,552 units
  * **Average Order Value (AOV):** $1.23K
* **Interactive Slicers:** Multi-dimensional filtering across Region, Year, Quarter, Month, Exact Date, Sales Channel, Product Category, and Customer Type (New vs. Returning).
* **Revenue Trend Analysis:** Line charts highlighting monthly sales volume and seasonal peaks throughout the fiscal year.
* **Segment Breakdown:** Visual comparative analysis across 5 geographic regions (*Central, East, North, South, West*) and 4 sales channels (*Online, Retail Store, Corporate Sales, Marketplace*).
* **Product Performance Tracker:** Categorical revenue breakdown alongside Top 5 highest-grossing products (led by Gaming Laptops at $432.5K+).
